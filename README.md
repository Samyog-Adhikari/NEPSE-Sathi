# NEPSE Sathi

## Problem Statement

Navigating the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) presents significant challenges for both retail and active investors:

1. **Fragmented Information**: Market data, live floorsheets, broker trading behaviors, company financial disclosures, and regulatory notices are scattered across disconnected portals, making comprehensive analysis slow and cumbersome.
2. **Information Asymmetry**: Institutional and experienced market participants have access to specialized tooling, while everyday retail investors lack access to structured technical screeners, order-book depth analysis, and broker accumulation tracking.
3. **Complex Financial Jargon**: Financial reports, quarterly earnings, and technical signals are often difficult to interpret without professional financial literacy, and quality tools rarely offer native Nepali language support.
4. **Scattered Portfolio Management**: Investors frequently juggle separate spreadsheets and manual calculations to track buy/sell costs, SEBON fees, capital gains taxes, and real-time unrealized gains or losses.

---

## The Solution We Are Building

NEPSE Sathi is an all-in-one financial intelligence and portfolio platform designed specifically for the Nepali market.

### Core Pillars of the Platform:

- **Unified Real-Time Market Hub**: Consolidates live indices, sector performance, market depth, and individual stock profiles into a single, high-performance interface.
- **Deep Market Analytics**: Provides automated technical indicator calculations (RSI, MACD, Moving Averages), live floorsheet transaction monitoring, and broker concentration analytics to reveal institutional market flow.
- **AI-Assisted Financial Explainer**: Uses artificial intelligence to synthesize complex technical signals, corporate filings, and quarterly reports into clear, contextual summaries that any investor can understand.
- **Bilingual Accessibility**: Full dual-locale support in both English and Nepali, making financial data and market tools accessible to a broader audience across Nepal.
- **Integrated Investor Toolkit**: Built-in portfolio tracking with automatic fee and tax calculations, trade journaling, IPO tracking, and custom watchlists in one place.
